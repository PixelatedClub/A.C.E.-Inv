# Uniform catalog basis

The clean catalog is intended as a comprehensive detachment inventory starting point, not as a replacement for cadre guidance or current uniform policy.

- DAFI 36-2903, Dress and Personal Appearance of Department of the Air Force Personnel, 29 February 2024, including current guidance memoranda: service dress / blues, OCP, outerwear, footwear, and physical training gear.
- DAFI 36-2903 AFROTC Supplement, 18 November 2021: AFROTC-specific OCP tapes, patches, and GMC/POC rank forms.
- AFROTCI 36-2010, Cadet Operations, current guidance memorandum: GMC ranks C/4C and C/3C; POC cadet officer ranks C/2d Lt through C/Col.

The catalog intentionally includes issued uniform components and commonly controlled accoutrements. Cadre should remove locally unissued choices or add detachment-specific property when necessary.

The official WINGS item-number index contains 493 unique numeric records. Seventy-two records that were absent from the original bundled index were added from the `Official Import` sheet in `Auburn Uniform Inventory Test.xlsm`; those rows retain their workbook source label in the database. The workbook contains no official records for men's Blues trousers in 39 Long, 40 Regular, 40 Long, or 41 Long, so A.C.E. does not guess item numbers for those sizes.

Male and female cadet officer cloth ranks are mapped to the official WINGS records numbered 867 through 878. Existing local "Soft Blues" inventory lines for C/2d Lt through C/Col are migrated to those records without changing their A.C.E. barcodes, quantities, or checkout history. The official index has no matching cloth records for GMC 100 (C/4C) or GMC 200 (C/3C), so those four sex-specific entries remain local and use "Cloth Blues" terminology.

Hard Blues ranks are the metal rank insignia. The official index contains mini-metal records 2348 for C/Col and 2349 for C/Lt Col, so those local Hard Blues entries map to the official records. No official metal records are present for GMC 100, GMC 200, C/2d Lt, C/1st Lt, C/Capt, or C/Maj; A.C.E. retains local entries for those grades rather than inventing WINGS item numbers. Rank migrations do not add text to inventory notes.
